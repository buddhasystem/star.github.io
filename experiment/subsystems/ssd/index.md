This site is the home of the STAR-SSD; its primary goal is to provide information to operate the detector and to analyse its data. See the Operations, Computing, Online and DATA sections below.

The detector was built by three laboratories: [Subatech](http://www-subatech.in2p3.fr/~hadrons/index.shtml){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}&nbsp;<span data-feather="external-link"></span> ([Nantes](http://www-subatech.in2p3.fr/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}&nbsp;<span data-feather="external-link"></span>), [IReS](http://ireswww.in2p3.fr/ires/recherche/alice/wwwalice.html){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}&nbsp;<span data-feather="external-link"></span> ([Strasbourg](http://ireswww.in2p3.fr/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}&nbsp;<span data-feather="external-link"></span>) and [WUT](http://hirg.if.pw.edu.pl/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}&nbsp;<span data-feather="external-link"></span> ([Warsaw](http://www.pw.edu.pl/english/index.html){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}&nbsp;<span data-feather="external-link"></span>). For information on the design and the production of the SSD consult the Hardware, Prod. DB and Documents sections.

Use the [SSD forum](https://www.star.bnl.gov/HyperNews-star/get/starssd.html){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}&nbsp;<span data-feather="external-link"></span> of the STAR hypernews to communicate with the SSD group.
