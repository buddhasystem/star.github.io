---
title: "Dr. Yixin Zhang defends Ph.D. thesis"
---
Please join us in congratulating Dr. Yixin Zhang from Shandong University, who, on August 29, successfully defended his Ph.D. thesis "Measurement of Transverse Single-Spin Asymmetry for Inclusive Jets and Hadrons within Jets in pp Collisions at RHIC-STAR." His advisor was Prof. Qinghua Xu. Yixin will continue as a postdoc at SDU. Congratulations Dr. Zhang, and many thanks Qinghua for your continued support in mentoring the next generation of physicists.
