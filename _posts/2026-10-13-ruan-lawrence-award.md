---
title: "Lijuan Ruan receives the 2026 Ernest Orlando Lawrence Award"
---
Please join us in congratulating Lijuan Ruan on being a recipient of the 2026 Ernest Orlando Lawrence Award. This prestigious award honors people "for their exceptional contributions to research and development supporting the U.S. Department of Energy's missions in science, energy, and national security." Specifically, Lijuan is honored for her "outstanding leadership and ingenuity in design, construction, and operation of novel detector systems in the STAR experiment, transforming dilepton final states into precision probes of heavy ion collisions." Congratulations, Lijuan!!
