---
title: "Lijuan Ruan named a 2026 Fellow of the American Physical Society"
---
Please join us in congratulating Lijuan Ruan who was named a 2026 Fellow of the American Physical Society. Her citation is as follows: "For innovative development of the STAR Time-of-Flight and Muon Telescope Detectors, and for the physics of identified hadrons, dileptons, and quarkonia in the study of quark-gluon plasma properties as RHIC." Congratulations Lijuan!!!
