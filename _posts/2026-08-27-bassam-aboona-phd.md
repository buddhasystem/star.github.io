---
title: "Dr. Bassam Aboona defends Ph.D. thesis"
---
Please join us in congratulating Dr. Bassam Aboona from Texas A&M, who has successfully defended his Ph.D. thesis, "Testing Factorization and Universality of the Collins Asymmetry using Transversely Polarized p(^)+Au Collisions at sqrt(s\_NN) = 201 GeV with STAR." His advisor was Prof. Carl Gagliardi. Bassam is looking for a job outside academia. Congratulations Dr. Aboona, and many thanks to Carl for your continued support in mentoring the next generation of physicists.
