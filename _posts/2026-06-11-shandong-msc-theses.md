---
title: "M.Sc. theses defended at Shandong University"
---
Please join me in congratulating Jinghua He, Chao Wang, and Sijie Zhang from Shandong University who successfully defended their M.Sc. theses on May 28. Jinhao He on "Measurement of the Transverse Polarization of Lambda Hyperons within Jets in pp Collisions at 510 GeV at STAR,” supervised by Prof. Qinghua Xu and Prof. Jinlong Zhang. Chao Wang on "Measurement of the transverse-momentum-dependent longitudinal single-spin asymmetry of W bosons in the RHIC-STAR experiment,” supervised by Prof. Jinlong Zhang and Prof. Qinghua Xu. Sijie Zhang on "Study of jet quenching in O+O collisions at sqrt(s\_NN) = 200 GeV by STAR,” supervised by Prof. Li Yi and Prof. Maowu Nie. Jinhao has decided to continue in industry, Chao as a teacher, and Sijie will continue as a PhD student at Yale. We wish them all the best of luck! Congratulations to Jinhao He, Chao Wang, and Sijie Zhang! Many thanks to Qinghua, Jinlong, Li, and Maowu for your continued support in training the next generation of physicists.
