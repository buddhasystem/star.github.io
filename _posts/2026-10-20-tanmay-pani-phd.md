---
title: "Dr. Tanmay Pani defends Ph.D. thesis"
---
Congratulations to Dr. Tanmay Pani from Rutgers University who, earlier this month, successfully defended his Ph.D. thesis, "Spacetime Evolution of Jets with the STAR Experiment." His advisor was Professor Sevil Salur. Congratulations, Dr. Pani! And many thanks to Sevil for your continued support in mentoring the next generation of physicists.
